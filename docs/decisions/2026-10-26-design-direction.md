# Design direction: Clear glass

**Date:** 2026-10-26
**Who decided:** mine

## Context

Three directions were shown: A Clear glass (light, Liquid Glass), B Night pulse (dark, bold, Whoop-like), C Quiet paper (warm, serif, editorial).

## Decision

A, Clear glass.

## Why

"More modern and feels cleaner. Feels like a tool that is supposed to do stuff for me and tell me this, and it's not opinionated."

## Alternatives

B Night pulse. C Quiet paper: closer to the owner's other apps, kept in mind but not chosen.

## What it means

The design system and every screen build on Mist, Ink, Leaf, Sky, Ember and Liquid Glass surfaces. See `docs/design/direction.md`.
