# Built for one person

**Date:** 2026-10-26
**Who decided:** mine

## Context

Interview, round 1: who the app is for.

## Decision

Vesel is for the owner only: one person, one Polar Loop, one iPhone.

## Why

It is a personal tool. Other users would bring accounts, support and privacy obligations for nobody.

## Alternatives

Building for all Polar owners (the old April 2026 vision). Dropped.

## What it means

No accounts, no sign-in, no App Store listing. Installed on the owner's iPhone with their Apple Developer account. Opening it to others later would need sign-in and a real server.
