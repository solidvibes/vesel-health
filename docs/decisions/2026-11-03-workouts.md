# Workouts: the Loop notices them, Vesel reads, shows and explains with them

**Date:** 2026-11-03
**Who decided:** mine (brainstorm 2026-11-03); the items marked agent's below were decided while planning
**Plan:** docs/plans/2026-11-03-workouts.md
**Supersedes:** the "Training plans and athlete load" line of `docs/intent.md` Out of scope, in part (per-workout effort is now in); the "Training sessions … are not read in v1" line of `docs/how-it-works/band-sync.md`

## Context

The Loop can notice a workout by itself (Polar's automatic training detection). Vesel left those sessions on the band, so a run or a ride showed up only as "high stress skipped" and a low-recovery morning had no reason given. The owner wants workouts read, shown, and used to explain stress, heart rate and recovery, without turning them into training stats.

## Decision

- Vesel switches on the Loop's automatic training detection and reads every finished workout on each read and when the Loop says a workout started or ended. Each is kept as it came off the band, plus typed rows. Workouts stay on the band: Vesel never deletes them.
- Automatic start and stop is the core and works with no GPS. When "Record my route" is on and location is allowed, the phone records its own route from the Loop's start to its end and joins it to the workout. No route: the workout is complete anyway. The route stays on the phone and the owner's Mac.
- Shown in Journal (cards between night and day), on Today (a short line when worked out today, and the recovery headline naming yesterday's workout when recovery is off), as purple blocks on the heart-rate and stress charts, and on a Workout page. Body gets nothing.
- Per-workout effort is in scope: Polar's training load, shown as Easy, Moderate or Hard with the number and its source.
- Stress readings inside a workout are not counted.
- The owner's fix to a workout's sport (or "Not a workout") is kept and never overwritten by a later read.

**Still never:** effort summed per week, a weekly load, bests, streaks, change arrows. No-score-to-chase (`2026-10-27-no-score-to-chase.md`) holds.

**Out of scope (later):** trimming a workout's length, starting a workout from Vesel, weekly totals, anything on Body.

### Made while planning (the owner confirms at the plan gate)

- "Record my route" is **on by default**, but iOS's location question appears only when the owner taps: the row shows "Needs location · Allow" until location is allowed. Opening a screen never asks. (Main thread, on the owner's behalf.)
- The route is drawn by Vesel as a plain line, with **no map service**. Showing it on Apple's map would send the route's area to Apple, a new service receiving location; that is an open question for the owner and gets its own decision only if the owner says yes. (Main thread.)
- Effort words from fixed cut-offs on Polar's number (Easy under 60, Moderate 60–149, Hard 150 and up; provisional, like the stress thresholds). (Agent's, assumed.)

## Why

Workouts are the most common reason for a high-heart-rate afternoon and a low-recovery morning; without them Vesel can't explain either. Reading them off the band keeps the no-cloud rule.

## Alternatives

Leaving workouts on the band (status quo: no explanation). Reading them through Polar Flow (cloud; rejected by `2026-10-26-no-cloud.md`). Weekly training load (athlete territory; still out).

## What it means

`docs/intent.md`: the Out of scope line on training load is narrowed and the Never list gains "never add up effort over weeks". Workouts stay on the Loop and use its storage (only a factory reset clears them); Loop & settings' storage warning names them. A route needs location permission "Always", because the Loop wakes Vesel in the background.
