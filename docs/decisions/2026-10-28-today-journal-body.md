# Three tabs: Today, Journal, Body

**Date:** 2026-10-28
**Who decided:** mine
**Supersedes:** 2026-10-27-night-tab.md (the four tabs), 2026-10-26-design-screens.md (screen map), 2026-10-27-night-stress-strip.md and 2026-10-27-day-calendar.md (where the strip and calendar live)
**Superseded in part by:** 2026-10-28-one-page-per-signal.md (Body's separate chart page)

## Context

Brainstorm 2026-10-28 (four rounds). Sleep, recovery and calm each showed on three of four tabs; heart rate and "past 7 days" on all four. The owner did not know where to look.

## Decision

Tabs are Today · Journal · Body (icons: sun, closed book, heart with a pulse).
- **Today:** the headline with a few sentences when needed, and three range lines: Recovery, Sleep, Calm. No charts. The Right now card leaves Today. Tapping a line pushes that thing's full page over Today; back returns.
- **Journal:** "what happened on that day". One day: its night (sleep, recovery) and its day (calm, heart rate). Week strip and the month calendar pick the day; each card opens the same full page Today opens. Nothing spans weeks. Room for adding things to a day later (a later idea).
- **Body:** "how am I changing". Weeks and months only, never one day: every signal (recovery, sleep, calm, HRV, resting and lowest heart rate, breathing, skin temperature) with its normal drawn as a band that moves, plus Heart fitness and About you. Loop & settings keeps only the band (pairing, sync, backup).
- The rule: a thing gets one short form (Today) and one full page; elsewhere it links. Never the same view three or four times.
- Week strip and month calendar: three dots per day (recovery, sleep, calm), each hollow when outside the normal.

## Why

Each tab answers one question, Today keeps the focus, and nothing is repeated.

## Alternatives

Today · Body only (home changes when looking back). Today · Journal with a Day | Weeks switch (the overlap again behind a switch). Four tabs cleaned up (overlap creeps back).

## What it means

Night, Stress and Trends tabs go; their content moves into the full pages, Journal and Body. Widget and deep-link targets change. Reversing means rebuilding the tab bar and splitting the pages again.
