# Full pages say what tends to help

**Date:** 2026-10-28
**Who decided:** mine

## Context

The owner: "if the HRV is low I don't know what I can do. This would be the holy grail of this app."

## Decision

A full page (Recovery, Sleep, Calm) has "What tends to help" under what went into it, tied to what was off, using the owner's own numbers where they fit (their usual bedtime). Labelled as general advice for most people, not medical advice. Later, with tags: "From your own nights", only counts from the owner's own nights, each tappable. On the page only, never a notification.

## Why

Turns "something is off" into something to do, without nagging.

## Alternatives

Only the owner's own patterns (nothing to read for weeks). No advice.

## What it means

A fixed, reviewed list of general tips per contributor ships with the app. Own patterns wait for the tags plan.
