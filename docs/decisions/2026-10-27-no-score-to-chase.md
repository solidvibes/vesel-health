# Never turn health into a score to chase

**Date:** 2026-10-27
**Who decided:** mine

## Context

The fitness-age plan asked whether heart fitness could be shown as an age. An age is the most chaseable number there is, and the owner already did not want Vesel to become a scoreboard (see `2026-10-27-in-range-rings.md`).

## Decision

Vesel never turns health into a score to chase: no streaks, no bests, no month-to-month arrows on worked-out estimates. Heart fitness is shown as a band (younger than, about, older than your age), never as an age.

## Why

A number you can push up invites chasing it; an estimate that is often off by 10 years should not be shown as one exact figure.

## Alternatives

Showing the fitness age (rejected). A monthly sentence only (kept for later if the band feels too chaseable).

## What it means

`docs/intent.md` Never list and `AGENTS.md` Non-negotiables gain the line. Estimates worked out from readings show no change from the previous period.
