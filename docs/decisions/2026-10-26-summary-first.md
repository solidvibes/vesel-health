# A short summary first, charts on demand

**Date:** 2026-10-26
**Who decided:** mine

## Context

Interview, round 2: "if I want to, I can see my data visually".

## Decision

The app opens to a short answer to "how am I doing?". Charts, history and detail are one tap away. Home-screen widgets show the headline numbers.

## Why

Most days a glance is enough.

## Alternatives

A dashboard of charts by default. Too busy for a daily glance.

## What it means

Every screen leads with a headline and one sentence; detail sits below or one level deeper.
