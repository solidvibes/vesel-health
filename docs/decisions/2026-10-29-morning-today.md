# Today in the morning: "I'm up" first, never the night before

**Date:** 2026-10-29
**Who decided:** mine
**Supersedes:** for last night only, "the last known numbers stay, faded" of `2026-10-28-reading-and-first-week.md`; the strip's "pages end on today" of `docs/how-it-works/days.md`

## Context

On waking, the owner had to scroll to find "I'm up", and Today showed the night before as if it were last night until last night arrived. It looked broken.

## Decision

- "Good morning — I'm up" is the first card on Today.
- Until last night is on the phone, Recovery and Sleep never show an older night. While the band still records it, in the 2 hours after it stops (Polar finishes the night up to ~90 minutes after waking), or while it is being read: "Processing…", with a "Processing last night" card where "I'm up" sits. Otherwise a dash and "Last night isn't in yet". The same holds on Journal and the Sleep and Recovery pages.
- Journal's week strip runs Monday to Sunday.

## Why

The time-bound action comes first, and Today never passes one night off as another. A fixed week puts each weekday in the same spot.

## Alternatives

Keep the old night with a small note (as before: missed). Week ending on today (as before).
