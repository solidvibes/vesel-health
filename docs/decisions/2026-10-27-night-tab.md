# The Sleep tab becomes Night: one page with sleep and recovery

**Date:** 2026-10-27
**Who decided:** mine

## Context

Recovery was a pop-up from Today, and Trends opened a different recovery page. The owner wants one place for it and no fifth tab. A first version put Sleep | Recovery behind a switch; the owner's redesign the same day dropped the switch.

## Decision

The Sleep tab is renamed Night and is one page: two rings inside each other (recovery outside, sleep inside) with a small legend, then sleep, then recovery, on one scroll. Today's recovery and sleep rings and Trends' Recovery open Night. The Recovery pop-up and the Past nights list go; earlier nights are picked from the calendar under the title (`2026-10-27-day-calendar.md`).

## Why

Sleep and recovery come from the same night, so they sit together; nothing is hidden behind a switch.

## Alternatives

A Sleep | Recovery switch (first version, dropped). Keep the pop-up and open it from everywhere (Recovery stays hidden).

## What it means

Changes the approved screen map (`docs/design/screens.md`): still four tabs, Today · Night · Stress · Trends.
