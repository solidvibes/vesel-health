# Vesel is published under Solid Vibes

**Date:** 2026-10-30
**Who decided:** mine
**Changes:** `docs/decisions/2026-10-28-open-source.md` (who is named as owner, pull requests, where the repo lives)

## Decision

- Vesel is named as "Solid Vibes, a brand of Stojanov Ventures UG (haftungsbeschränkt)". The UG stays the legal owner, because a brand can't own rights.
- The repo moves to the `solidvibes` GitHub organization.
- The CLA also lets the UG pass the rights on, for example to a buyer of Vesel. Pull requests stay closed until the CLA check is set up.
- Loop & settings credits the Polar BLE SDK and the other libraries, says the measurements come from Polar, and says Vesel is not a medical device. So does the README.
- Polar's feature name is written "Polar's Nightly Recharge" in titles and alerts.

## Why

If Vesel is ever sold, one owner holds every right and nobody has to work out who owns which part. The Polar SDK licence asks for its copyright notice in the product itself.
