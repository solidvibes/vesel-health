# One units switch; waist only explained under heart fitness

**Date:** 2026-10-28
**Who decided:** mine

## Context

The owner wants imperial units available, and found waist shown "a bit random without explanation".

## Decision

- About you (now in Body) gets a Metric / US segmented switch, styled like the biological sex switch. Metric by default. It covers weight (kg/lb), height and waist (cm/ft in), skin temperature (°C/°F). Stored values stay metric.
- Waist is no longer shown as a reading. It appears only in Heart fitness' "How it's worked out" with: "Waist helps estimate your heart fitness. It stays on this phone." Heart fitness stays a band against your age, never an age (`2026-10-27-no-score-to-chase.md`).

## Why

Usable for American owners; every number explains why it is there.

## Alternatives

A choice per unit (more rows, rarely needed). Drop waist for BMI.

## What it means

Formatting goes through one units setting; reversing is a display change only.
