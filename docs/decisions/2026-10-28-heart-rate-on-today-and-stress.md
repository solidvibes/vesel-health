# Heart rate back on Today; the third line is Stress

**Date:** 2026-10-28
**Who decided:** mine
**Supersedes:** the "Right now card leaves Today" line of `2026-10-28-today-journal-body.md`; the "stress ring means calm" naming of `2026-10-27-in-range-rings.md`

## Context

After using the new tabs on the iPhone: the live heart rate is about now, so it belongs on Today, not the Journal. And with range lines, more is worse on the third line, so "Calm" read backwards.

## Decision

- Today: the Recovery · Sleep · Stress card first, then a Heart rate card: the live bpm with its pulsing dot, today's resting heart rate, and a small line of today so far. The reading card is the last card.
- Journal's Heart rate card shows the day as a chart, no live number. Each Journal card shows that day at a glance: sleep stages with bedtime and wake time, recovery's line, stress by the hour, heart rate through the day.
- The third line, page, chart and widget row are called **Stress** everywhere (Garmin's and Polar's word). Positive wording such as "a calm day so far" stays.
- Opening the app within 5 minutes of the last read reads nothing; only live heart rate connects. Exception: the band is still recording last night. The reading list shows "Last night" only while last night isn't on the phone yet.
- No dashed underlines under titles; titles still open their meaning when tapped.

## Why

Today answers "how am I now"; the Journal answers "what happened that day". A line where more is worse should carry that name.

## Alternatives

Keep Calm (reads backwards on a line). Strain (Whoop's word for training load, a different thing).

## What it means

Rename across app, widgets, docs and glossary. Reading behaviour changes only when a read starts and what the list shows; what is fetched, stored and cleared from the band does not change.
