# Health data never goes to anyone else's cloud

**Date:** 2026-10-26
**Who decided:** mine

## Context

Interview, round 2: privacy.

## Decision

Data moves only band → iPhone → the owner's Mac mini. No Polar cloud (AccessLink), no Apple Health or iCloud, no outside analytics or crash reporting.

## Why

Fewer parties holding health data. Also removes the dependency on the Polar app syncing first.

## Alternatives

Polar AccessLink (needs Polar Flow to sync, data in Polar's cloud). HealthKit as a hub (Polar data would pass through Polar's cloud first). Both rejected.

## What it means

The Loop is paired with Vesel instead of Polar Flow (one peer only; switching back needs a factory reset). Any new service that receives data needs a new decision.
