# Direction

**A · Clear glass.** Approved 2026-10-26.

## Mood

Airy · Calm · Clear. A modern tool that tells you things, not an opinionated one.

## Palette

| Name | Hex | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Mist | #F3F3F1 | Background |
| Ink | #111113 | Text and numbers |
| Leaf | #2F9E5B | Recovery |
| Sky | #3B6CF6 | Sleep |
| Ember | #E8742A | Stress |

Cards are Liquid Glass over soft blurred colour.

## Type

SF Pro Display for headings, SF Pro Text for body (the system font).

## Liked and rejected

- Liked: A's cleanliness and modern feel. C's warmth matches the owner's other apps (Runde), but it felt more opinionated.
- Rejected: B (dark, Whoop-like) and C (warm paper, serif).

## Voice

Owner, 2026-10-28: positive, candid and honest, never negative.

- A good day is said as good news ("Everything looks great today."), with a short encouraging line. Never as the absence of bad news ("Nothing is off today").
- When something is off, say it plainly, then what tends to help. No alarm, no blame.
