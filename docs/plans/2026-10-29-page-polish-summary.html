Order of work

  1. Loop button turns orange below 20 %.
  2. Band data report: the screen-behind-the-bar fix (wider only once your screenshot shows another screen).
  3. The swipe test, your yes, then whole-page swipes and the title in the bar.

Decisions

  1. Yours: the five changes, in one plan.
  2. Built once in the shared signal page; Heart fitness gets the title in the bar too.
  3. Orange is the app's existing stress colour; no new colour.
  4. Paging stops at your first day of data and at today.
  5. A new page opens at its top. Day and week remember their own date, as now.
  6. Only the page on screen loads live data; neighbours fill in when they arrive.
  7. Changes an earlier plan: a right swipe mid-page used to go back; now it pages. The edge swipe still goes back.

Risks

  1. None in the sensitive areas: nothing leaves the phone, nothing is deleted.
  2. The swipe may fight iOS's swipe-back. Shows as: back doesn't work. Caught by the test before building.
  3. Heart rate 3M is heavy. Shows as: lag when swiping back many months. Checked by swiping back 10 pages.

How we'll know it works

  1. Automatic tests for which periods exist and the low-battery state.
  2. On the Simulator: each picture above, light and dark, largest text and VoiceOver.
  3. Only you, on your iPhone: swipe a few days back and forth on Heart rate; swipe-back still works.
  4. Only you: the orange ring when the Loop is really below 20 %.
