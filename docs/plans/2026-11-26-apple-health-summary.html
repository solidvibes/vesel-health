Apple Health Plan

Vesel copies your readings into Apple Health so Mira can read them. Look at the boxes and the settings pictures. The numbered lists are there if you want them.

What you getYour readings in Apple Health

Heart rate, resting heart rate, sleep with stages, breathing and steps come first. HRV, calories and workouts follow in a second update. Your whole history goes in, newest first.

What you seeOne switch in Loop & settings

Off until you turn it on. Apple asks which data to allow. A line under it says how far it has got. Nothing changes on Today and nothing pings you.

Check thisGuessed sports go in as "Other"

Vesel sometimes guesses a workout's sport from speed and your past workouts. A guess can change later, so only the Loop's sport or one you picked goes to Health. Everything else is "Other".

Your one stepPut the Loop first, three times

Your iPhone counts steps and calories too. In Health, set Vesel first under Data Sources for Steps, Active Energy and Resting Energy, so nothing is counted twice.

I assumedHRV over sleep, one a night

Worked out from short beat recordings taken while you sleep, matching how Apple measures HRV. Vesel's own HRV page doesn't change.

Decisions
  1. Apple Health and its encrypted iCloud backup allowed mine
  2. What gets written, and what doesn't mine
  3. HRV worked out Apple's way mine
  4. All history mine
  5. Loop first in Data Sources mine (calories added by me)
  6. HRV: short recordings during sleep, one value a night agent's
  7. A small table of what was written agent's hard to undo
  8. Fixes replace the old copy in Health agent's hard to undo
  9. How each reading is named in Health agent's hard to undo
  10. Workout heart rate and calories sit with the workout, not the day agent's
  11. Guessed sports written as Other agent's
  12. Turning the switch on again rewrites everything, with no doubles agent's
  13. Two updates: simple readings first, then HRV, calories and workouts agent's
Risks
  1. Health data goes to a new place: Apple Health and iCloud. Shows as data in Health, which is the point.
  2. One new database table. If it goes wrong, the app won't open after the update. Tested before it ships.
  3. Vesel deletes its own older copies in Health, and only its own. If wrong, a day could disappear from Health. Vesel keeps its own copy either way.
  4. Polar's calorie figures might not split the way I assumed. That would show as calories a bit too high or low in Health.
How we'll know it works
  1. The checks on the Mac and in the Simulator, with a fake Health
  2. In the Simulator: turn it on, see the data in Health, fix a night, see no doubles
  3. You: on the iPhone, readings show in Health and in Mira
  4. You: after a locked night, it catches up with no doubles
  5. You: check that Polar's day calories include workouts
