2 · Steps, in three places

Steps on Today, in the Journal and in Body

The band already sends the day's step count with every read. It is stored but not shown yet. No goal, no streak, no best: just the number, and in Body your normal.

Today · a small card under Heart rate
Today's count so far, with the time of the last read. No "usual by now": comparing a half-day to a whole day invites chasing.
Body · a Steps card
Steps per day for 4 weeks over your normal (shaded, moves with you). Tap opens the full chart: week, month, 3 months.

Journal · each day's card

You dropped hourly steps on 27 October. A steps-by-hour chart on the Journal card would bring that back, so it is drawn both ways. Without it, the card just gets one line.

J1 · One line Recommended
The day's total, next to what the card already shows. Keeps your 27 October call.
J2 · With steps by hour
Small bars per hour under the total. Brings hourly steps back, and makes every Journal card taller.
