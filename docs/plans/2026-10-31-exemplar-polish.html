Decisions

  1. Apache 2.0; contributors still give their rights to your UG. yours
  2. Eight steps, one pull request each. agent's
  3. Morning part 1 first; parts 2–3 after C2. agent's, needs your OK
  4. The fake band is compiled only into test builds; a check fails the real build if it sneaks in. agent's
  5. Each screen's model gets only what it uses. No extra framework. agent's
  6. Data won't open: a calm screen, never delete or reset, retries by itself. agent's · hard to undo · touches your data
  7. Words on the start screens (drawn above). agent's, needs your OK
  8. Words on the "Couldn't show this" card. agent's, needs your OK
  9. No licence header in every file; one LICENSE and NOTICE file. agent's
  10. GitHub checks run on GitHub's Macs, no secrets needed. agent's
  11. Code formatting goes last, in one commit, so it doesn't clash with other work. agent's
  12. Optional: lock screen widgets hidden while locked; server log line made safe. agent's recommendation

Risks

  1. App start changes: a mistake could stop the app working in the background after a restart. Covered by a test and your phone check.
  2. Fake band move: a mistake could break the Simulator. Shows as a failed check.
  3. Rewiring every screen (C2) could change what a screen shows. Shows in the new tests and previews.
  4. Not touched: anything leaving the phone, the phone ↔ Mac link, database changes, deleting data, pairing.
