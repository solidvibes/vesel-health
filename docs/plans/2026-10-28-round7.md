# A softer background and steps

**Status**: Implemented 2026-10-28

- `BlobBackground`: one faint wash at the top right (sleep blue 0.10, recovery green 0.07); `warm` glow 0.18.
- Today: `StepsCard` under the Heart rate card; "so far · read HH:mm"; opens the Steps chart.

- No migration, nothing stored, nothing leaves the phone.

## Decisions
1. **mine** — Background B, much softer: one faint wash at the top right; `warm` quieter (0.18).
2. **mine** — Today: a small Steps card under Heart rate: steps so far with "read HH:mm"; no goal, no comparison; opens the Body steps chart.
3. **mine** — Body: a Steps card near Stress: per-day bars over the moving normal (`PersonalRange`, last 4 weeks), today's bar lighter; chart page with the same ranges as the other Body charts.
4. **mine** — Journal J1: one "Steps" line with the day's total; no hourly bars.
5. **agent's** — The "read" time is `band_ledger.stored_at` of today's `dailySummary` row: the time that summary was last stored, which is the time the band was last read for steps. `activity_day` has no update time.
6. **agent's** — 0 steps counts as no steps (the Loop wasn't worn): no row, no bar, and not part of the normal.
7. **agent's** — Steps get their own Body group "Movement" (right under Stress) so the "Recovery, sleep and stress" heading stays true; the Steps term is a new glossary entry in its own "Movement" group.
8. **agent's** — The shaded normal behind the steps bars uses the accent colour (as in the mockup); the bars use the muted text colour.

## Deviations
- The chart page offers the ranges the other Body charts have (4 weeks, 3 months); the mockup's "week" does not exist for any Body chart.
- Journal's Steps line is its own small card in the Day section (Journal's cards are per signal, not one card per day); it opens the Steps chart ending on that day.
- Body's Steps card shows its normal under the chart ("Your normal 7,100–11,300 a day. Today's bar is lighter: the day isn't over.") instead of above it, so the shared Body card stays unchanged.
- Today's read time comes from the ledger, not from `activity_day`; when no ledger row exists the card says "so far" alone.
- `WidgetBackground` is unchanged: its 78% surface layer now sits over an almost flat background, so widgets read as nearly plain.
- Steps alone don't start Body (it still says "No data yet" until the first night).

## Verification

- VeselStorage `StepsObservationTests`: today's steps and the last read time (two reads, the later wins); a past day has its own steps and no read time; 0 steps and no row mean none; Body gets one point per day with steps, with the read time; the series reaches back as far as the other trends.
