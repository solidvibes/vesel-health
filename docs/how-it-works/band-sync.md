# Band sync

**Status:** Built: syncs while the app is open, background wake-ups (band events, background app refresh) and live heart rate. "I'm up" on Today (phase 4) and the "Sync Vesel" Shortcut (phase 6) are Built. Background wake-ups are checked on the owner's iPhone; the Shortcut is not yet. Today's reading list ticks off each part as it arrives. Readings with data are stored as each kind arrives, small kinds first, and opening the app isn't held back by a cut-off read; the Loop's check-ins and a long read while charging also read, and Sync now and I'm up keep going after you leave (Built, `docs/plans/2026-10-29-sync-stays-fresh.md` phases 1, 2 and 4; keeping going after you leave not yet checked on the owner's iPhone).

> Vesel collects the Loop's data in the background, stores it on the phone, then clears it from the band.

## Intent
Works without opening any app (intent: "Never depend on the Polar app being opened"). The Loop has 15 MB, so data must be cleared after it's stored.

## How it works
- **What is read:** sleep (stages, times), Nightly Recharge, 24/7 heart rate (5-min averages; every second during high activity), 24/7 beat-to-beat (PPI) sessions, skin temperature (5-min), activity samples (steps, MET, activity classes) and the daily summary, which gives the day's steps, active time and calories. Today's steps card says "read 14:32": that is when the band's daily summary for today was last stored (`band_ledger.stored_at`, kind `dailySummary`), so the number is as fresh as the last read. A read that finds nothing for a day never replaces steps already stored. Each day is kept as it came off the band, plus typed rows. **Workouts** (`workouts.md`): every read lists the Loop's workouts and reads new or changed ones, between skin temperature and heart rate; the one still running waits for its end; one that fails is skipped without stopping the read; they stay on the band (never cleared); the Loop's automatic training detection is switched on once. Sleep score and skin-contact files are not read yet and stay on the band; Loop & settings warns when band storage passes 60 %.
- **When it runs** (iOS decides; there is no fixed timer):

  | Trigger | What happens |
  |---|---|
  | The band reconnects, finishes a night, or asks to sync (Built) | iOS wakes (or relaunches) Vesel for a short time to sync; a reconnect syncs only if the last sync that reached the Loop started more than 5 minutes ago. A night ending or a sync request during a read waits for it, then reads afresh. |
  | A workout starts or ends (Built, `workouts.md`) | The Loop's exercise notice says started or ended: a check-in like below. Planned: the phone's route recording starts or stops |
| The Loop checks in (Built) | Its inactivity alert (or the warning before it), an exercise starting or ending, or a battery or charging change (also once on every reconnect) wakes Vesel; it reads if the last sync started more than 5 minutes ago and no check-in tried in the last 5 minutes, one check-in at a time. How often these come is not known; none is on a schedule. Logged as "your Loop checked in" |
  | Long read while charging (Built) | A background processing task iOS runs when the phone is idle and charging, usually at night, at most once an hour; it gets minutes instead of seconds and catches up heart rate and beat-to-beat. It waits for a running read, then reads afresh. Logged as "long read while charging" |
  | Background app refresh (Built) | iOS picks the moment, maybe a few times a day, maybe not at all; never sooner than 30 min after the last ask |
  | App opened (Built) | Sync if the last read that finished ended more than 5 minutes ago (a read that was cut off or failed doesn't hold the next one back), also after iOS ended the app (what the band said about sleep at the last sync is kept on the phone), or every time unless the band was last known to have finished the night (nothing known yet, after forgetting the Loop, or a last sync that seems to be in the future because the clock went back: it reads). Live heart rate connects either way |
  | Sync now on the Syncing page (Built) | Immediate |
  | After pairing (Built) | Immediate |
  | "Sync Vesel" Shortcut (Built) | An App Intent that runs in the background without opening Vesel, e.g. a personal automation "When my alarm is stopped → Sync Vesel". It asks the band whether it still records sleep; if so (or it can't say) it ends the night, then syncs. It answers "Last night: 7 h 12 m, recharge OK", "Polar is still finishing last night" or "Your Loop isn't nearby". iOS gives it tens of seconds; an unfinished sync is picked up by the next one |
  | "I'm up" on Today (Built) | The tap is kept (across launches) and any read, whatever started it and never held back by the 5-minute gap, first asks the Loop and ends the night unless it already stopped, then syncs; logged as its own trigger. Loop not nearby → kept for the next read, for up to 6 hours; the Loop near but refusing → dropped. The 2 hours Polar needs start when the Loop got the tap (`docs/plans/2026-10-30-calmer-morning.md`, phase 1; not yet checked on the owner's iPhone) |
  | Sync now and I'm up keep going (Built) | Started by a tap (Sync now on the Syncing page, Today's retry, Forget this Loop's last read, I'm up), the read carries on after you leave Vesel. It waits for a running read, then reads afresh, first waiting for the Loop on Vesel's own background time. Only once the Loop answers does iOS show "Reading your Loop" with progress (per part, so a long heart-rate read can sit still and iOS may end it) and a cancel; every ending closes it, and a card left from an earlier launch is withdrawn at launch. A cancel is like any cut-off read. If iOS doesn't start it, the read carries on as it is |

- Force-quitting Vesel from the app switcher stops background wake-ups until it's opened again.
- In the background iOS gives Vesel only a short time. If it runs out, the sync stops, nothing is cleared from the band, and the next sync picks up where it left off. Readings with data are stored as each kind arrives, so a cut-off read keeps them. What clearing relies on (empty days, and heart-rate and beat-to-beat days) is recorded only after a whole read: every part came back, it wasn't stopped, and the Loop never dropped the connection during it. The Polar SDK reports a dropped connection as "no data" or as part of a day. After a day the Loop wasn't worn, newer days of the same kind wait for one whole read; after that they show as they come. The database is woken at the start of each background sync and put to sleep at the end, so iOS never ends Vesel for holding it.
- Polar finishes sleep and Nightly Recharge up to ~90 minutes after waking. Opening Vesel or any automatic sync never ends the night; only a kept "I'm up" tap (delivered before a read) or the Shortcut do, and the Shortcut no longer ends it on "doesn't say" once last night is stored. While the band still reports sleep (or doesn't say), sleep isn't fetched yet.
- **Clock changes:** the band keeps the clock offset Vesel set at the last sync; it knows nothing of summer time. Each reading is stored with the offset it was in: sleep, Nightly Recharge and activity times come from Polar as exact moments; heart rate, beat-to-beat and skin temperature come as clock times, read in the phone's time zone at that moment. When the clocks go back, the band keeps summer time until a sync sets it back, which shows as its times stepping back; the repeated hour is read twice, in order. When the clocks go forward, a reading the band still timed in winter time after 03:00 (before the next sync) lands an hour early; nothing in the band's data shows when it was set forward. Days stored by earlier versions keep one offset per day and are read as before; none of the owner's real data falls on a clock-change day.
- **Order:** tell the band a sync starts → set its clock → battery and storage → sleep + Nightly Recharge (once the band has stopped recording sleep) → daily summary → activity samples → skin temperature → heart rate → beat-to-beat, readings with data stored as each arrives → if the read was whole: empty days and heart-rate/beat-to-beat days recorded → clear from the band → tell the band the sync ended. Heart rate and beat-to-beat come last because each downloads every 24/7 file on the band, so a backlog makes both slow.
- **What is fetched:** each kind from the day before its last stored day through today, or from an earlier day that still has to be read again (below); the first sync starts the day before pairing. One sync at a time; a second trigger waits for the running one.
- **Clearing:** per band data type, only through the last day whose data of that type was fully stored after the band's midnight flush; never today or yesterday. A day that came back empty counts as stored only once it came back empty on two syncs after it ended: the Polar SDK reports some failed reads as "no data", and one empty read can't tell that from a day the band wasn't worn. The last two empty reads must be at least an hour apart. Such days are read again on every sync until they are settled, so days not worn never block clearing for good. A sync that stops partway clears nothing and keeps the readings with data it stored; a band type clears a day only once every kind it covers is stored for that day, e.g. heart rate and beat-to-beat. A day already stored with data is never replaced by an empty re-fetch.
- **Forget this Loop** marks everything still on the band as cleared (the factory reset wipes it), so a new Loop's clearing starts fresh.
- **Loop & settings** shows connection, battery, a Syncing row (last read, opens the Syncing page) and band storage used; above 60 % it warns "Band storage is filling up".
- **Low battery** (Built): the battery stored at each read drives the ring at the end of the tab bar: below 20 % it turns the stress colour, VoiceOver adds "Battery low" and the Battery row reads "18 % · Low". It shows the battery as of the last read: it changes only when a read stores a new value (charging changes wake a read).
- The phone's database is excluded from iCloud Backup.
- **Reading list (Built):** only the first read after the app comes to the foreground gets it, and only if that read is still running after about a second (`ReadingCardGate`, VeselCore). Later reads while the app stays open and background reads never show it and never change or fade Today; only the ring button in the tab bar turns. While it shows, Today keeps its last numbers, faded, with the list as its last card: "Loop found", "Heart rate", "Last night · 2 of 3 parts" (sleep, Nightly Recharge, skin temperature; left out while the band still records the night, and when last night was already on the phone as the read started: the Loop is still asked, the row just isn't shown) and "Stress today". A part gets a tick when it is in, the one being read a spinner (the heart-rate row a green pulsing dot and "Heart rate · reading"; Reduce Motion stops the pulse), the rest an empty ring. No bpm is shown. Each part is stored as it arrives, so Today's faded numbers may update during the read. When the read ends the list folds away (no animation with Reduce Motion) and VoiceOver says "Reading done". `BandSync` reports each step after it happens and never changes the order of fetch, store and clear; the status holds counts only.
- **Live heart rate (Built):** while Vesel is open on screen, the band streams heart rate about once a second; the stream pauses during each sync and comes back after. Shown in Loop & settings (also on Today's Heart rate card and on today's Heart rate page, in its Now row), never stored or logged; the band's 24/7 export is the stored history. Stops when Vesel leaves the screen. Vesel never switches the band to SDK mode (live heart rate doesn't work there).
- Every call to the band runs one at a time, a sync, the live stream and pairing included.
- Every read first asks to connect if the Loop isn't connected, then waits for it: up to 60 seconds when a tap or opening Vesel started it, 20 seconds in the background. Meanwhile Today shows "Waiting for your Loop · Reads as soon as it's near your phone."; "Can't reach your Loop" with Try again shows only after Sync now or Try again ran out. A try that never found the Loop doesn't hold back the next automatic read (the 5-minute gap counts only reads that reached it), so the Loop connecting reads at once. After a drop the Polar SDK's own reconnecting can give up; without asking again, reads failed as "Loop not nearby" until Vesel was restarted. After iOS relaunches Vesel, the SDK can restore the Loop's link without reporting it, so asking again does nothing: when nothing is heard within 3 seconds, the read closes that link by the Loop's Bluetooth address (remembered on the phone at each connection) and opens it afresh. A read stopped while it waits is stored as stopped early, not as not nearby; it still shows as "Loop not nearby".
- **A read never hangs (Built):** it stops when the Loop drops during it (its drop count changes, or it stays away more than 15 seconds) or when a band call goes 90 seconds without the band answering (a fetch that answers day by day keeps going; the heart-rate and beat-to-beat downloads are one request over every 24/7 file, so they get 10 minutes). It ends as "didn't finish", like a cut-off read: nothing cleared, and a call left hanging makes no further band calls. Asking the Loop about sleep and ending the night stop the same way. A trigger that arrives while a read is giving up on the Loop reads afresh.
- **Connection log (Built):** every change in the link to the Loop (connecting, connected, disconnected with the Polar SDK's reason, Bluetooth off or on) is stored with its time, kept 14 days (`band_link`, migration v5). Times and words only, no health values, not uploaded; debug builds list the last 2 days under "Connection" in the band data report.
- **Band data report (debug builds only):** in Loop & settings, per hour for the last 3 days: beat-to-beat sessions, intervals per session, share flagged, heart-rate samples, and whether the live stream is on. Counts only, no values.
- Every sync is logged (trigger, start, end, outcome) for the Syncing page.
- After the numbers are worked out, each sync checks the alerts (`alerts.md`).

## Syncing page (Built)
- Opened from Loop & settings' "Syncing" row (it shows the last read) and from "Last read at … ›" ("Syncing ›" before the first read) in Today's "Can't reach your Loop" card. Nothing new on Today.
- Shows: last read; when the last tries failed, "Vesel hasn't reached your Loop since 08:12. It tried 3 times."; Sync now (moved here from Loop & settings, with its error card); "If it's stuck" (close Polar Flow, Bluetooth on and close by, don't swipe Vesel away, Sync now and keep it open about 30 seconds, turn Bluetooth off and on); recent syncs, the 20 newest, in plain words.
- Starts: you opened Vesel, your Loop asked, your Loop checked in, long read while charging, iOS woke Vesel, Shortcut, Sync now, I'm up (its own trigger, `imUp`; older "I'm up" rows stay Sync now), after pairing. Endings: done, partly done, Loop not nearby, Loop didn't answer, stopped early, no Loop paired, didn't finish, and "reading now…" for the newest row while a sync runs (known only from the app's live state; any other row without an ending was cut off and reads "didn't finish"). "It tried N times" counts finished tries only. Forget this Loop's last sync shows as Sync now. The stored detail is never shown as it is; the log holds no health values and isn't uploaded.
- "Nothing is lost for now: your Loop keeps its readings until Vesel has stored them, as long as its storage doesn't fill up." Vesel clears only what is on the phone; how many days the Loop can hold is not known.

## Do / Don't
- Don't promise a sync interval; say "when iOS wakes the app". Nothing on the Loop reads on a schedule; for fixed times, the "Sync Vesel" Shortcut in a personal time-of-day automation.
- Don't clear anything from the band that isn't stored on the phone.
