# Days and range lines

**Status:** Built: one page per signal (Day, Week, 4 weeks, 3 months), range lines (the rule, the lines on Today and the widgets, three dots per day in the strip and the calendar, sleep far above your usual counts as outside) and the picked day, which only Journal shows. Plans: week strip, week-strip feedback, tabs lines, tabs layout. First-week checks on Today (tabs voice plan). Planned (`docs/plans/2026-11-04-recovery-sleep-polish.md`): marks instead of hollow dots on the Recovery, Sleep and signal pages, and three new pages (In bed, Efficiency, Bedtime · wake); see `recovery.md` and `sleep.md`. Today, the widgets, the lock screen, Journal and Body keep the range lines and hollow dots.

> Each line shows your own normal as a shaded stretch and a dot for the day: filled inside it, hollow outside it. Tap Journal's title to pick any earlier day; Journal then shows it. Today is always today.

## Intent
See at a glance whether a day was off, and look back at any day, without turning health into a score to chase (decisions `2026-10-28-range-lines.md`, which replaced `2026-10-27-in-range-rings.md`, and `2026-10-27-day-calendar.md`).

## How it works
- **A line is inside or outside, not a grade.** The track runs between two ends, your normal is shaded on it and the day is a dot: filled inside, hollow outside. The line never fills; no best day, no streaks, no celebrations. Too little and too much both count as outside, except where only one side can be a problem.

| Line | Your normal | Outside | Track |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recovery | Polar's OK to very good (4 to 6) | below, when Polar says compromised or worse | 1 to 6; the dot sits at indicator + sub-level ÷ 100 |
| Sleep | from 1 SD under to 2 SD over the mean of the previous 28 nights | below the bottom, above the top | one spread (the width of the normal, at least 1 h) either side of the normal, never under 0 |
| Stress | up to the allowance | above it | 0 to twice the allowance |

- **Recovery:** Polar's Nightly Recharge as-is. OK, good or very good is inside; compromised, poor or very poor is below. Polar already compares each night with your usual, so its normal is fixed; sleep and stress use the last 4 weeks.
- **Sleep:** time asleep against your normal. A night under the bottom (mean − 1 SD) is below; a night beyond mean + 2 SD is above (about 1 night in 40, only really long nights), and counts as outside like below does.
- Durations are written without empty minutes: "2 h", "7 h 05 m", "45 m".
- On Today the three lines share one glass card.
- **Stress** (the stress line, titled "Stress" everywhere; `calm` in the code): the day's high-stress minutes against your usual for the previous 28 days that count. A day counts only with at least 10 clean readings; a barely-worn day shows no stress dot and doesn't shape your usual. Inside at or under mean + 1 SD, never under 15 minutes; above that, outside. Stress has only a top, recovery only a bottom.
- A dot past either end of the track sits at that end (still hollow).
- **Learning:** (everywhere it is worded, the line is "Your usual range appears after 7 nights · N of 7", or for stress "Your stress levels appear after 7 days · N of 7"; no count on the first night) fewer than 7 earlier nights (recovery, sleep) or 7 earlier days with high-stress minutes (stress): no line, the learning words instead. Stress needs the stress baseline first, so it learns for about two weeks after the band is first worn: while the baseline is still learning, a day with at least 10 clean readings shows the learning words too (its explanation counts the baseline's days so far).
- **No data** for that day: the faint empty track, no dot.
- Each line's state has a "how it's worked out" sentence with its numbers (method `inRange`, version 2). Worked out when the screen reads; not stored, so nothing is recomputed. Where the normal and the dot sit comes from VeselCore; screens and widgets only draw it.
- Today's three lines, the widgets and the three dots per day all follow this rule. Each Today row says its value on the right: Polar's word, hours asleep, the stress word now. Each row opens its page for today (Recovery, Sleep, Stress). A page shows the same line for its own day. VoiceOver: "Sleep, 7 h 40 m, above your normal".
- **First week on Today (`firstWeek` v1):** while a line learns its normal, its range line is replaced by a row: the line's name, "3 more nights · Sun" (the morning its normal first shows; stress "about 9 more days", no weekday), and one 15 pt circle per night or day, filled with a tick in the line's colour when it came in, an empty ring when the Loop missed it or it is still to come. Stress's row is 14 long and wraps. One line under: "A night the Loop missed leaves its check empty and the row one longer." Never a streak. A row goes when its line has its normal.
- **Three dots per day:** recovery, sleep, stress, side by side, in Journal's week strip and calendar. Filled inside, hollow outside, a small muted dot while learning, an empty slot without data. VoiceOver: "Thursday 22 October, recovery inside your normal, sleep above your normal, stress inside your normal".
- **Picking a day:** tap Journal's title. A month calendar opens (weeks start on the phone's first weekday; ‹ › for other months, back to the month of your first data). Each day shows its three dots. Days before your first data and after today are greyed and can't be picked. With the largest text sizes the month shows as a list of days instead of a grid. "Today" goes back; the sheet is tall enough for every week row, so the button never covers a day.
- **Week strip:** Journal shows, once there is data, seven days under the title: weekday letter, date and the day's three dots. Each page is one week, Monday to Sunday, so a weekday always sits in the same spot (Thursday fourth); days after today show greyed. The page shown is the one holding the picked day; picking a day on it never moves it, and at midnight it moves with today. Tap a day to pick it; swipe left or right for another page, back to the page of your first data and forward to today's. Days before your first data or after today are greyed and can't be picked. The strip and the calendar share the picked day, so each follows the other. VoiceOver: each day is one button with the calendar's words, the picked one selected; "Earlier week" and "Later week" are actions on each day, offered only when that page exists (and the scroll gesture). Reduce Motion: the week changes without a fade. At the largest text sizes the weekday letters drop and the date shrinks to fit.
- **One picked day,** Journal's. When a new day starts, it goes back to today (at midnight while the app is open, else the next time it comes to the front). The day is held to your first day with data … today. The Simulator opens Journal on a day with `-VeselDay 2026-10-20` and on its calendar with `-VeselCalendar`. Whenever the picked day changes, Journal reads again for it. Today and the pages never read it: a page gets its day in its route, so the same page opened from Today (today) and from Journal (the picked day) shows the right day.
- **The night shown:** each day shows its own night only, today included: an older night never stands in for last night. Before last night is in, it is "on its way" while the band still records it, in the 2 hours after it stops (Polar needs up to ~90 minutes), or while it is being read. A tapped "I'm up" still waiting for the Loop also counts as on its way (up to 6 hours). Then Today shows an "Evaluating your night" card ("Nothing for you to do. We'll let you know when it's ready."; with the morning report off "Nothing for you to do. It shows here when it's ready.") with Recovery and Sleep saying "Evaluating…", Journal's Sleep and Recovery cards say "Evaluating…", and the Sleep and Recovery pages show "Evaluating your night". Otherwise Today shows a dash with "Last night isn't in yet. It fills in on the next read.", and Journal and the pages say no sleep is recorded yet. Once last night is stored, the morning is over: no "I'm up", no "Evaluating", whatever the band says later (Built, phase 1 of `docs/plans/2026-10-30-calmer-morning.md`). The calendar and charts stay per day.
- **Steps** are a count, not a line: no range, no goal, no verdict. Today shows today's steps so far under the Heart rate card with the time the band was last read for them, and today's bars per hour so far under it (like the Heart rate card's line; none until there are steps by the hour); Journal's Day section ends with a Steps card for the picked day (the total, "so far" for today, and small bars per hour); both open the Steps page (below); Body has a Steps card (below). Steps come from the Loop's daily summary (`activity_day`); a day with 0 steps (the Loop wasn't worn) counts as no steps: no card on Journal, no bar on Body, and it doesn't shape your normal. Body's normal for steps is the same as every measure's: the mean ± 1 SD of the previous 28 days, from 7 days on (`DailySteps.normal`, never below 0, written "7,100–11,300 a day" to the nearest 100). Today's bar is drawn lighter: the day isn't over. No best day, no streak, no goal line, no change arrows.
- **The pages** (`PageRoute(kind, day, period)`; `docs/plans/2026-10-28-one-page-per-signal.md`): Recovery, Sleep, Stress, Heart rate, Steps, HRV, Resting HR, Lowest HR, Breathing, Skin temperature, In bed, Efficiency and Bedtime · wake each have one page, the same from Today, Journal and Body (In bed, Efficiency and Bedtime · wake are opened from Sleep, not from Body). Title, a D · W · M · 3M switch (VoiceOver: Day, Week, 4 weeks, 3 months), a header, one chart, then on D the day's cards. The header on D is the day's number with its date ("12,481 steps · Tue, Oct 27"); on W, M and 3M a plain average a day or night with the date range (Heart rate: the lowest to highest over the days shown; Recovery: the most frequent verdict, newer night wins a tie). Averages count only days with a number, and Steps leaves today out (the day isn't over). A finger on the chart replaces the header with the touched bar or point and its time or date; lifting restores it. Hold a finger on the chart for a moment, then drag, to read; a sideways drag anywhere under the switch, or ‹ ›, moves one period (a day on D, 7, 28 or 90 days on W, M, 3M, windows ending on today, › off at today, ‹ off before the first day with data); a tap on a day in W, M or 3M opens D on it. Switching D to W, M or 3M shows the window holding that day, and back to D keeps the day. The swipe from the screen's left edge goes back; the title and the switch stay still while the period under them slides. A light tick marks each new bar the finger reaches, each period change and each day opened from W, M or 3M; the glances on Today and Journal scrub at once and tick nothing. Today and Journal open a page on D for their day; Body's cards open it on M. W, M and 3M draw the moving normal as a shaded band behind each night's or day's own point (Heart rate has no band). The simulator opens a page with `-VeselPage recovery|sleep|stress|heartRate|steps|hrv|restingHeartRate|lowestHeartRate|breathing|skinTemperature|inBed|efficiency|bedtime` on the launch tab, `-VeselPeriod d|w|m|3m`, and `-VeselScrub 14:00` for a finger on the first scrubbable chart.
- **D charts:** Steps, the hourly bars ("14:00–15:00 · 960 steps", "no steps"); Heart rate, the 5-minute line with sleep shaded; Stress, the hourly bars ("14:00–15:00 · High, 72"); Sleep, the stages with the overnight heart rate ("03:12 · Deep · 51 bpm"); Recovery and the overnight signals, the 7 nights ending on the shown night with that night drawn full and the others faded (a night with one value has nothing finer to show). VoiceOver: each chart is one adjustable element that steps by the hour (bars) or 15 minutes (lines, stages), by day on W, M, 3M, with a "Show this day" action there; ‹ › are buttons ("Earlier day", "Later week"). Steps' hours come from the per-minute step samples kept as they came in `raw_export` (`activitySamples`), read for the shown day only and summed by wall-clock hour (`HourlySteps`): on a day of 23 or 25 hours there are still 24 bars (the hour that repeats is one bar, the hour that is skipped has none). An hour with no steps has no bar. Today's bars can add up to a little less than the total, since the daily summary and the samples are read separately; they are not reconciled. No busiest hour, no goal, no best. Decisions: `docs/decisions/2026-10-28-one-page-per-signal.md`, `docs/decisions/2026-10-28-hourly-steps.md`.
- **Workouts (`workouts.md`):** Journal shows a Workouts section between Night and Day, one card per workout that started on the picked day, in time order, each opening the Workout page. Today shows one short line per workout started today, under the Steps card. A workout is not a line: no range, no dot, nothing in the week strip or calendar.
- **Journal on a past day:** the title is the date ("Thu, Oct 22") with a "Back to today" ink pill beside it; the Night line under its heading always names the evening the shown night began ("Monday night, October 26"); a missing night says "No sleep recorded this night"; the Stress card's value is the day's high-stress time ("45 m", "—"). The cards open the pages for that day.
- **Signal pages, D:** HRV, Resting heart rate, Lowest heart rate, Breathing and Skin temperature lead with a picture of the night against your normal (`NormalScale` in VeselCore places it; the mark's colour fills the dot), then the 7 nights' line graph, then "How it's worked out": where your normal comes from, the term's "Good or bad" sentence and the numbers folded. A normal worked out on read (Lowest HR, In bed: `range28`; Efficiency: `range28Low`, version 1) shows its own method beside the night's, on D and in W, M and 3M's "How it's worked out".

## Do / Don't
- Don't turn a line into a score: it never fills, and no end of it is "best".
- Don't show a line's state without its explanation one tap away.
- Don't make the title look like a button; it still is one for VoiceOver.
