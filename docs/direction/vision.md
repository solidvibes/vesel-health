# Vision

Direction, not commitment.

A quiet health assistant that lives on the phone. It reads the Polar Loop in the background, knows what "normal" looks like for its owner, and speaks up only when something is off. Open it and you see today first: how you slept, how recovered you are, how stressed you've been. History is one tap away.

The wearable is just a data source. The Loop today; an Apple Watch or another band later, through the same door.

The Mac mini at home keeps a full copy and is where heavier analysis can grow: longer-term trends, better stress models, maybe asking questions about your own data — all without anything leaving the house.

Later, maybe a second person (a partner with their own Loop). v1 is single-user with no accounts; adding people would mean sign-in and a per-person split of the data.
